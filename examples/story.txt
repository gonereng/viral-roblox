I was at the gym trying to look like an absolute elite athlete on the treadmill, running at maximum speed with my wireless earbuds blasting hype music. 
Feeling unstoppable, I decided to pull off a casual, mid-sprint water break.
I unscrewed my giant metal water bottle, took a big gulp, and instantly inhaled water straight into my lungs.
A violent coughing fit erupted, sending a fountain of water across the control panel. 
My feet completely lost the rhythm of the belt, and before I could grab the handrails, the treadmill launched me backward like a human catapult. 
I flew off the track, hit the rubber floor with a horrific squeak, and slid straight into the leg press machine where three gym bros were watching in total silence.
I laid there on my back, soaked in my own water, and gasped. 
"Just... checking the friction on the floor." 
I rolled into a tactical crawl, grabbed my bottle, and walked out without ever coming back.
